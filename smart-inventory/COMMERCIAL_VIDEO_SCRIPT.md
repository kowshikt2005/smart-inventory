# Smart Inventory Commercial Video Script

## Version A: 60-Second Main Commercial (English)

### Campaign Theme
From business chaos to confident control.

### Target Audience
Small and mid-sized Indian distributors, wholesalers, and operations-led businesses.

### Tone
Fast, practical, trustworthy, India-market focused.

---

## Scene-by-Scene Script (60s)

### Scene 1 (0-6s)
Visual: Busy warehouse office. Team members juggling spreadsheets, WhatsApp chats, and paper invoices. Red stock warning sticky notes on screen.

Voiceover:
"Orders are coming in. Stock is moving out. Payments are delayed. And your team is still chasing everything manually."

On-screen text:
"When business grows, chaos grows faster."

---

### Scene 2 (6-12s)
Visual: Hard cut to Smart Inventory dashboard loading quickly. Clean metrics cards update in real time.

Voiceover:
"Meet Smart Inventory. Built for Indian businesses that need speed, control, and GST-ready operations."

On-screen text:
"Smart Inventory"
"GST-ready. Operations-ready. Growth-ready."

---

### Scene 3 (12-20s)
Visual: Sales order opens. Partial quantities are invoiced. Order status changes from OPEN to PARTIALLY INVOICED.

Voiceover:
"Ship part now, bill part now, and complete later. Partial invoicing keeps every order accurate, without breaking your workflow."

On-screen text:
"Partial Invoicing"
"OPEN -> PARTIALLY INVOICED -> FULLY INVOICED"

---

### Scene 4 (20-28s)
Visual: Stock scan runs. Reorder suggestions appear automatically. One reorder converts into multiple vendor purchase orders.

Voiceover:
"Running low on stock? Smart Inventory auto-detects shortages and creates reorder flows by vendor in seconds."

On-screen text:
"Auto Reorder Intelligence"
"From stock shortfall to vendor PO"

---

### Scene 5 (28-36s)
Visual: Customer payment is entered. System allocates payment to oldest invoices first. Outstanding ledger updates live.

Voiceover:
"Receive one payment and automatically clear oldest dues first with FIFO allocation. Better collections, cleaner books."

On-screen text:
"FIFO Payment Allocation"
"Cleaner receivables. Better cash flow."

---

### Scene 6 (36-45s)
Visual: GST reporting screen. B2B/B2C split, tax breakup, and return-ready export views.

Voiceover:
"From GST classification to return-ready reports, your compliance workflow stays consistent and audit-friendly."

On-screen text:
"GST Workflows Built In"
"B2B/B2C | CGST/SGST/IGST"

---

### Scene 7 (45-53s)
Visual: Role permission matrix and secure login moments. Manager, accountant, billing operator each sees role-specific panels.

Voiceover:
"Role-based access keeps teams focused and secure. Everyone sees what they need. Nothing more."

On-screen text:
"Role-Based Access Control"
"Admin | Manager | Accountant | Billing"

---

### Scene 8 (53-60s)
Visual: Owner looks at dashboard confidently as orders, invoices, and payments flow in one unified system.

Voiceover:
"Smart Inventory. One platform for sales, purchase, stock, ledger, and GST. Stop managing chaos. Start scaling confidently."

On-screen text:
"Smart Inventory"
"Run faster. Stay compliant. Scale smarter."
"Book a demo"

---

## Voice Direction Notes
- Pace: 145 to 155 words per minute.
- Voice: Warm, confident, business-focused (male or female).
- Accent: Neutral Indian English.
- Music: Modern business pulse, starts tense, becomes optimistic by Scene 3.
